My Hobbies

The things I enjoy when I'm away from the screen.

💻

Programming

I enjoy programming because it allows me to turn my ideas into something real and useful. I like experimenting with different programming languages, solving problems, and building small projects while continuously improving my skills.

🎮

Gaming

Gaming is one of the ways I like to relax and enjoy my free time. I especially enjoy competitive and strategy-based games because they challenge my thinking, improve my decision-making, and make every game feel different.

📚

Reading

I enjoy reading about technology, science, personal development, and interesting ideas. Reading helps me explore perspectives beyond my classroom and keeps me curious about the world around me.

🎵

Music

Music is something I turn to when I want to relax, focus, or simply enjoy my time. I listen to different genres depending on my mood, and I often find music to be a great way to refresh my mind after a busy day.

👨‍💻

Typing

I enjoy computer typing because it helps me express my ideas quickly and efficiently. I like improving my typing speed and accuracy while working on assignments, coding projects, and other tasks. For me, typing is not just a skill but a useful part of working comfortably with technology.
