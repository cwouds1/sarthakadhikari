05 — MY PERSPECTIVE

What I Think

Ideas, opinions and perspectives that shape who I am.

01

Technology & The Future

As an 18-year-old aspiring computer engineer, I see technology as a powerful tool that can reshape the future. I believe artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, robotics, and software will transform how we live and work. However, technology should be developed responsibly, ethically, and for the benefit of humanity. I want to be part of creating innovative solutions for tomorrow’s challenges.

02

Education

I believe education is more than just getting good grades or passing exams. It teaches us how to think, solve problems, and understand the world around us. I see education as a lifelong journey that builds confidence, curiosity, and character. In the future, I hope education becomes more practical, creative, accessible, and connected to real-life challenges.

03

Debates and Communications

I believe debates and communication help us express ideas while also learning to understand different perspectives. Debating has taught me to think critically, listen patiently, and speak with confidence. Good communication is not only about making others understand us, but also about understanding them. I believe these skills are essential for personal growth and a successful future.

04

Nature and Environment

In my eyes, nature is something that's deeply connected to our lives and future. Climate change, pollution, deforestation, and the loss of biodiversity remind us that our actions have consequences. I believe protecting the environment is not only the responsibility of governments but also of every individual. Small choices, when made collectively, can create meaningful change. As technology advances, I hope we learn to develop sustainably while respecting and preserving the natural world for future generations.

Think deeply. Build boldly.
